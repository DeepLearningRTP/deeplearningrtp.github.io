DLRTP Weekly — Decision Models in Practice

Wed Nov 18, 2026 · 12:00 PM EST · Frontier RTP (Tarheels conference room), 800 Park Offices Dr, Durham, NC

Decision models in practice, with Scott. "Decision model" is the new name for models that choose among answers instead of generating text. Scott will explain what the term covers, then show a real case: on his Dog Walking Database project, Google's Decision API turned hundreds of questionable categorizations into automatic assignment that's about 95% accurate. He'll cover: * Why he picked Google's API over OpenAI's * What it took to build * What's still unsolved
