2026-11-18

Decision Models in Practice

Decision models in practice, with Scott.

"Decision model" is the new name for models that choose among answers instead of generating text. Scott will explain what the term covers, then show a real case: on his Dog Walking Database project, Google's Decision API turned hundreds of questionable categorizations into automatic assignment that's about 95% accurate.

He'll cover:

  • Why he picked Google's API over OpenAI's
  • What it took to build
  • What's still unsolved

2026-07-22

Emergent Sparsity in Frozen-Random-CNN Deep RL (Scott Norton)

Playing Pong through 3 neurons: freeze a randomly initialized CNN, train a deep RL agent on top, and the agent spontaneously compresses all task-relevant information through as few as 1-3 of the 64 neurons in its first fully-connected layer, with no sparsity objective anywhere. Scott Norton presents his manuscript (arXiv pending) documenting the phenomenon in Atari Pong. No RL background required; the setting is informal and open to everyone. Find us in the Eagles room off the main coworking area.
